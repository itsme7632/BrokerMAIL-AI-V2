Implement a campaign cooldown visibility feature. This is a UI/UX enhancement only. Do NOT modify the existing campaign sending logic, quota calculations, cooldown logic, scheduler, SMTP handling, queue processing, or any backend functionality that is already working.

### Goal

When a campaign enters `cooling_down` status, users should clearly see:

* Why the campaign is paused
* How many emails were sent in the current quota window
* The mailbox hourly limit
* The exact remaining time until the campaign can resume
* The estimated resume timestamp

### Campaign Card Enhancements

For any campaign with status `cooling_down`, display a dedicated cooldown section:

Example:

Cooling Down

Hourly quota reached.

Quota Usage:
45 / 45 emails

Window Reset In:
42m 18s

Estimated Resume:
Jun 7, 2026 — 3:45 PM

Status:
Waiting for full quota window to clear

### Countdown Requirements

* Countdown must update live every second on the frontend.
* No page refresh required.
* Display format:

  * 59m 12s
  * 12m 03s
  * 01m 08s
* When countdown reaches zero:

  * Show "Checking quota availability..."
  * Then wait for backend status update.
* Never automatically change campaign status from the frontend.
