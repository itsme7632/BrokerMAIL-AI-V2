- Email Queue: 3516 active

This causes inaccurate queue statistics and incorrectly affects Platform Health.

- Remove those jobs from the email queue.

- pending
- queued
- processing
- scheduled
- retrying
- paused
- sent
- failed
- cancelled

- queued
- scheduled

- processing

- cancelled
- removed
- sent
- failed
- expired
- campaign deleted
- orphaned records

- Stop campaign

- Refresh Platform Health immediately

- explain why
