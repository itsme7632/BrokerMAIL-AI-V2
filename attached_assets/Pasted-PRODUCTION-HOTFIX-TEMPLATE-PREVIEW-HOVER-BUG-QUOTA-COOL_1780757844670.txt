=====================================

Issue:

Example:

45/45 sent
↓
Cooldown starts
↓
1 hour later
↓
Provider reports:
40 used
5 available
↓
Campaign immediately resumes
↓
Sends 5
↓
Quota exhausted again
↓
Cooldown
↓
Sends 4
↓
Cooldown
↓
Sends 3
↓
Cooldown

This causes quota probing and increases delivery failures.

====================================================
NEW REQUIRED BEHAVIOR
=====================

When a campaign reaches quota:

45/45 sent
↓
Enter cooling_down status

After cooldown expires:

Check provider usage.

Calculate:

available = quotaLimit - usedThisHour

If available < quotaLimit:

DO NOT resume campaign.

Remain in cooling_down status.

Check again later.

Only resume when:

available === quotaLimit

Example:

usedThisHour = 0
available = 45

Resume campaign.
