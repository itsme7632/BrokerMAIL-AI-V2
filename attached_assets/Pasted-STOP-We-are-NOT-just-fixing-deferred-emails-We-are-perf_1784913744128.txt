==================================================
PROBLEM
==================================================

Observed behavior:

A campaign was started.

The SMTP provider reached its hourly quota.

Instead of pausing immediately, BrokerMAIL AI continued processing the queue.

Over 100 emails became Deferred / Failed because of quota exhaustion.

This should NEVER happen.

==================================================
EXPECTED BEHAVIOR
==================================================

When quota/rate-limit is detected:

• Stop sending immediately.
• Do NOT continue processing the remaining queue.
• Put the mailbox into Cooling Down.
• Put the campaign into Cooling Down.
• Leave all remaining leads Pending.
• Start the recovery scheduler.
• Probe after the configured cooldown.
• Resume automatically only after quota is available.
• Resume from the exact next unsent lead.

One quota event must stop the worker immediately.
